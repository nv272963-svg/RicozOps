Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about becoming a Ricoz franchise partner and growing your business with confidence.

Who can apply for a Ricoz franchise?

−
Entrepreneurs, business owners and professionals looking to start or expand a service-based business can apply. Prior industry experience is beneficial but not mandatory.

What support does Ricoz provide?

+
Ricoz provides technology, marketing, operations, training and partner success support.

Is training provided before launch?

+
Yes. Complete onboarding and training are provided before launching your franchise.

How do customer leads reach franchise partners?

+
Qualified customer enquiries are assigned through the Ricoz platform based on your service category and location, ensuring fair and efficient lead distribution.

Can I operate multiple service categories?

+
Yes. Eligible franchise partners can expand into multiple business categories after meeting the required operational standards and performance criteria.

How long does the onboarding process take?

+
The onboarding process generally takes 7–15 working days depending on document verification, training completion and operational readiness.

Our franchise team is here to help you understand the opportunity, investment requirements and growth potential.
